About Mayondo Wood and Furniture Company

PRODUCTION UNIT – At our factory, located 1 hour outside Kampala, we employ over 50 employees. We have technical experts who specialize in curing timber, design, hardwood furniture, painting and staining, and the use of wood alternatives.

SHOWROOM – In our showroom, located in Kampala Industrial Area, we are staffed with quantity surveyors, design experts, engineers, architects, project managers, and other professionals to make your experience seamless.

The work starts in the minds of our clients and is met by phone, email, or an in-person meeting, then passed on to our drafters who create and design renderings, floor plans, and drawings for our customers.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, locally crafted furniture that combines functionality, comfort, and timeless design — while empowering our community through sustainable wood practices and employment opportunities.

Our Vision

To become Uganda’s most trusted furniture brand, known for excellence in craftsmanship, innovation, and customer satisfaction.

Our Values
